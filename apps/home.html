OfflinePay demo

An offline payment SDK and API that digital wallets plug in. PayA and PayB are mock wallets.

Demo story

  1. Aarav (PayA) enables Offline Pay and loads Rs 1,000 into the vault.
  2. Tea House (PayB) also enables Offline Pay.
  3. Both go offline. Tea House creates a request; Aarav scans, approves, shows his code; Tea House scans it → verified.
  4. Attack: tamper with the amount → rejected. Snapshot → pay → roll back → pay the Lodge → looks valid offline.
  5. Go online → the dashboard settles the real payment and catches the double-spend.
